A single sheet a designer can hand to a client: the mark, both lockups, the
colours and the clear space.

## Scope

- A multi-artboard composition layer that places mark, horizontal lockup and
  stacked lockup on one page.
- Clear space computed from the mark's real outline.
- The palette rendered as swatches with its values.
- Registered as one more exporter, using the composition layer.
