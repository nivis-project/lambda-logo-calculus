Read `reference/trefoil-type.html` and write down what it actually does, as specs. Record what it renders as a fixture, so the port has something to be proved against.

The prototype is the only authority on behaviour until this milestone is archived. Nothing is reimplemented here; it is read, described and recorded.

## Scope

- Specs covering the prototype's parameters, its nesting mathematics, its glyph construction and its rendering, each written from reading the source rather than from memory.
- The hidden couplings named: a parameter that silently drives another is a decision, not an accident, and it goes in the spec.
- The clamps and safety limits named, with the values the prototype uses.
- A recorded fixture of the prototype's own output across a matrix of settings, driven through its real controls in a browser.
- A stated tolerance for the parity comparison, derived rather than chosen.
