# parity Specification

## Purpose
The prototype's own output, recorded, so the port is compared against the thing
itself rather than against somebody's reading of it.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The recording is made by running the prototype

The fixture SHALL be produced by loading `reference/trefoil-type.html` in a real
browser and driving its own controls. No part of the prototype SHALL be
reimplemented to produce it.

A recording made by reimplementing the prototype proves only that two
transcriptions of it agree, which is the one thing nobody needs to know.

#### Scenario: Producing the fixture

- **WHEN** the fixture is regenerated
- **THEN** the prototype is loaded and driven, and its rendered output is read
  back from the page

#### Scenario: One command

- **WHEN** someone wants to regenerate the fixture
- **THEN** one command does it, and the prototype is the only source

### Requirement: The recording stores what the controls actually took

For every setting in the matrix, the fixture SHALL store the value each control
actually holds after being set, read back from the control itself, alongside the
value it was asked for.

A test SHALL fail when the two differ, so a slider that snapped to its step is
never silently compared against the unsnapped value the port would compute.

#### Scenario: A value that snapped

- **WHEN** a control is asked for a value its step does not allow
- **THEN** the fixture records what it actually took, and a test reports the
  difference

#### Scenario: A value that did not snap

- **WHEN** every control took the value it was asked for
- **THEN** the asked-for and actual values agree throughout the fixture

### Requirement: The container width is fixed and recorded

Because the prototype's geometry depends on the width of the element it draws
into, the recording SHALL fix that width and SHALL store it in the fixture.

A fixture without its width is not a recording of anything reproducible.

#### Scenario: The width is in the fixture

- **WHEN** the fixture is read
- **THEN** it names the container width the recording was made at

#### Scenario: Reproducing a recording

- **WHEN** the recording is regenerated at the stored width
- **THEN** the output matches the previous recording

### Requirement: The matrix is wide enough to be worth something

The recording SHALL cover a matrix of settings that varies the amplitude, the
rotation, the fit size, the copy count and the stroke ending, and SHALL include
glyphs of every structural kind: a bowl, a stem, an arc, a diagonal, a
descender and a dot.

A test SHALL assert the recorded matrix meets those counts, so the fixture
cannot quietly shrink to the one case that happens to pass.

#### Scenario: The matrix covers its range

- **WHEN** the fixture is read
- **THEN** it holds several distinct amplitudes, rotations, fit sizes, copy
  counts and endings

#### Scenario: The matrix cannot shrink

- **WHEN** the fixture is reduced to fewer settings than the stated minimum
- **THEN** a test fails

#### Scenario: Every kind of glyph

- **WHEN** the recorded text is read
- **THEN** it exercises bowls, stems, arcs, diagonals, descenders and dots

### Requirement: The tolerance is derived, not chosen

The tolerance for comparing a port against the recording SHALL be derived from
the prototype's own rounding, and the derivation SHALL be written down beside
the number.

The prototype rounds every coordinate it writes to two decimal places, so a
coordinate can be out by up to 0.005 in each axis however correct both sides
are. That is a distance of `sqrt(2) * 0.005`, about 0.00707 font units, and it
is the floor below which no comparison means anything.

The tolerance SHALL be 0.02 font units: about three times that floor, and tight
enough to catch an error of one five-hundredth of a stroke width.

#### Scenario: The floor

- **WHEN** the tolerance is read
- **THEN** it is accompanied by the rounding it is derived from

#### Scenario: A tolerance that would be meaningless

- **WHEN** a tolerance below the rounding floor is proposed
- **THEN** it is refused, because no implementation could meet it

### Requirement: The fixture is committed, not regenerated by the gate

The fixture SHALL be a file in the repository. The gate SHALL NOT need a browser
to run.

The prototype is frozen and its digest is checked, so a recording of it is as
current as a fresh run would be.

#### Scenario: The gate runs without a browser

- **WHEN** the gate runs
- **THEN** no browser is launched and the fixture is read from the repository

#### Scenario: The prototype moved

- **WHEN** the prototype changes
- **THEN** its digest check fails first, which is the signal that the fixture
  has to be re-recorded

### Requirement: The port is compared against every recorded setting

The comparison SHALL run the port at each recorded setting and compare what it
draws against what the prototype drew, within the derived tolerance.

Every glyph of every pass of every recording SHALL be compared. A comparison
that covers a subset SHALL report which settings it skipped and why.

#### Scenario: Every setting

- **WHEN** the comparison runs
- **THEN** it covers every recording in the fixture

#### Scenario: Within the tolerance

- **WHEN** the port is compared against the prototype
- **THEN** every compared point is within the tolerance

#### Scenario: The margin is reported

- **WHEN** the comparison passes
- **THEN** it reports the worst difference it measured, so the margin is known

### Requirement: The comparison can fail

A test SHALL prove the comparison is capable of failing, by moving a coordinate
by more than the tolerance and confirming it is caught.

A comparison nobody has seen fail is a comparison nobody should believe.

#### Scenario: A nudged coordinate

- **WHEN** one coordinate of a recording is moved by one font unit
- **THEN** the comparison fails and names the setting and the glyph

### Requirement: What is compared, and what is not

The comparison SHALL cover the geometry the prototype writes as path
coordinates: stroke outlines, bowl rings and join loops.

It SHALL NOT cover the shapes the prototype writes as references to a
definition with a transform, because resolving those would mean reimplementing
the prototype's own drawing, which is what the recording exists to avoid. What
is left out SHALL be stated rather than left for a reader to notice.

#### Scenario: What is covered

- **WHEN** the comparison runs
- **THEN** it compares stroke outlines, bowl rings and join loops

#### Scenario: What is not

- **WHEN** the comparison runs
- **THEN** it states that shapes written as references are not compared, and why

### Requirement: Parity is a one-time gate

Once this comparison has passed and been archived, golden snapshots SHALL become
the baseline, and the prototype SHALL stop being authoritative for anything
except reading.

The parity suite SHALL stay in the repository as the record of the port.

#### Scenario: After the port

- **WHEN** a later change moves the geometry
- **THEN** it is the golden snapshots that notice, not the parity comparison
