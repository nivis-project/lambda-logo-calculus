## Context

What is true that forces a choice. The constraints, the pressures, and what
happens if nothing is decided. Write it so someone who joins in a year can tell
why this was a question at all.

## Decision

## Consequences

What follows from it: what becomes easy, what becomes hard, and what cost is
being accepted on purpose. A consequences section with only good news is a
consequences section that has not been written yet.

is not a preference: say what it would have cost or failed to do.
